Tablet and phone

  • Tablet rail (≤ 900 px): the Work group adds three icons under the views, with a divider. Work items detail becomes the flyout again.
  • Phone (≤ 640 px): today's bottom bar has four views plus Settings. With A it needs grouping: Timeline · Calendar (Day/Work week/Week behind a segmented control) · Work · Time · Review. Settings moves into a header menu. That is a real change to the phone shell.
  • Phone writes: action buttons in the bottom sheet. The confirm dialog becomes a full-height sheet whose confirm button names the effect.
  • Browser build: actions are desktop-only (no actions over HTTP), so Work, Time actions and Review are hidden there. Time totals still work if worklogs are collected.

States

  • Empty: “No items match. Try Not mine or Done”, with the JQL that was sent shown when the JQL toggle is on.
  • Loading: row skeletons like the timeline's. The detail keeps the old item, dimmed, until the new one arrives.
  • Errors: Unreachable or RateLimited shows an inline banner in the list with Retry (and “retry in 20 s” when Jira sends a wait). NotAllowed is never an error state: the view isn't shown.
