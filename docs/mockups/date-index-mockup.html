Timeline day index

A day rail beside the timeline: hover to preview, click or drag to jump, and the pill follows the day whose header is stuck at the top. Fake data. Use the controls to switch the range length, the breakpoint and the theme.

Range Width Theme Motion

Try

  • Scroll the list: the pill tracks the stuck day header.
  • Hover the rail: neighbours magnify, the bubble previews the target day.
  • Drag on the rail: the list scrubs day by day. Empty days snap to the nearest day with events.
  • Tab to the rail: ↑ ↓ day, PgUp PgDn week, Home End, Enter focuses that day's first event.
  • Search “ACT-” or “sync”: unmatched days dim on the rail, the range stays.

Density

  • Full (≥ 17 px per day): a number for every day with events, dots for empty days.
  • Sparse (6–17 px): numbers on Mondays and today, dots elsewhere. Hover reveals the numbers near the pointer.
  • Dense (< 6 px, long ranges): a bar per day, its length set by the event count. Month labels and the bubble carry the dates.
